# Anatomy & Histology Group Project Rubric

Unit: ANAT3008 / Histology & Anatomy Collaborative Practical
Scale: 1–5 per criterion. Downstream systems MUST convert scores to percentages
and MUST NOT display raw counts or tallies.

## Knowledge Application
Applies anatomical and histological knowledge to the practical case.

## Practical Skills
Competence in specimen handling, microscopy, and lab technique.

## Team Contribution
Equitable participation in dissection, documentation, and review.

## Scientific Communication
Accuracy of figures, labels, and written practical reports.

## Professionalism
Lab safety, ethics, and respectful collaboration.
